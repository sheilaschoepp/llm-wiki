# audit

Audit is the judgement pass. It asks whether the notes are good thinking objects.

## Purpose

Structural lint can say whether a page has the right sections. Audit says whether a concept/entity page is one idea, understandable, supported, and useful, and it earns the `verified` stamp by fact-checking the page against its raw source.

`verified` here means fact-checked against the raw source and passed. It is not the Ahrens-style human re-voicing gate that the foundation design (`a-archive/reference/smart-notes-llm-wiki-integration.md`) assigns to promotion. This wiki deliberately relocates that safeguard: pages stay LLM-drafted, and the defence against AI-voice prose lives in the ingest note-quality packet plus audit's own semantic-tell check (Step 4), not in a human rewrite at promotion. Audit's autonomy is therefore a conscious departure from that document's human-commit-point model, authorized by `CLAUDE.md`. Do not weaken the Step 4 tell-check or the ingest packet without re-introducing a human re-voicing step in their place.

## Verification Model

The scope, marker, and status rules every step below relies on, defined once here. The full runtime statement is `CLAUDE.md` → Page Status and Bullet Markers; this is audit's operating summary.

Five load-bearing terms:

- `verified_hash:` — the SHA-256 of a page body with canonical `> - *[unverified]* …` claim lines excluded. Marker-shaped text in inline/fenced code, HTML comments, or any other position remains semantic and hashed. The stamp guarantees the page's unmarked content is byte-identical to what was last fact-checked: immutability since the check, not correctness of the check. Re-confirm correctness with `full` mode.
- `*[unverified]*` — a claim-level marker on a non-obvious claim added since the last fact-check (or one whose backing image was re-extracted without a text change), pending a raw re-check. Write it immediately after the canonical `> - ` callout-bullet prefix. A *changed* claim demotes the page rather than riding it, so a marker on a `verified` page is an addition, not an edit. It is the unit `partial` mode re-checks, and the hash excludes its canonical line so it can ride a `verified` page as the pending delta.
- `*[tentative]*` — a claim-level marker for weak or thin support. Unlike `*[unverified]*` it is an epistemic judgement, not a process state: a fact-check does not clear it, and the two can co-occur on one claim. It is the safe-failure marker audit falls back to whenever the raw cannot positively settle a claim's support — mark `*[tentative]*` and set `needs-update` rather than delete or self-stamp.
- authored-tier worklist — the Warning-tier findings lint detects but leaves for audit to carry out. Audit's authored worklist *is* lint's Warning output, so a Warning check lint adds later automatically becomes audit's job with no edit here.
- durable bullet evidence — the exact-valid locator/entailment pair and per-claim terminal row defined in `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/verification-ledger.md`. It certifies only its exact bullet/locator/raw/context generation. It never substitutes for audit's fresh whole-page pair or preserves a page stamp across a support change.

**Scope by mode.** `partial` (default) includes every non-verified page plus every `*[unverified]*` claim on an otherwise-`verified` page and any page current defects/raw invalidation bring into scope. It may reuse exact-valid bullet pairs and backfills only missing/invalid pairs; every scoped page still pays two fresh page readers. Marker-free verified pages remain outside partial scope. `full` includes every maintained source, entity, concept, and synthesis page and deliberately distrusts prior bullet evidence: every required claim receives a new locator/entailment pair (`reused = 0`) plus both fresh page readers. Use `full` for a broad criterion change, a rushed prior audit, or periodic deep confirmation. A verified page whose cold re-check holds keeps its hash/date unless a correction or marker clearance requires a terminal write.

**Verification is terminal for content-changing work.** The `verified` stamp earned from raw verification is the last content-verification write a page receives. Every content-changing edit — audit's own Step 4 findings, lint's Step 1 authored worklist, and every cross-page edit — enters this run's fact-check scope and lands before Step 8. A bounded change on a hash-stable page may use **delta-certify**: rerun both bullet roles for every changed/new/revoked/dependent claim, retain only exact-valid unaffected rows, and run both fresh whole-page roles over the final page and raw manifest before re-stamping. An unbounded change reruns the complete page claim inventory. If the edit cannot be certified this run, including a new or relabelled locator anchor, apply the safe determinate fix when possible, set `needs-update` with a precise reason, strip the stale hash, and carry no process marker. Masking and later-audit deferral are not dispositions. The sole no-raw lane is the closed verification-neutral allowlist in Step 4a. The hard invariant: **a page never keeps or earns `verified` while a content-changing item is unapplied or its final body is uncertified.**

Step 4b's positively-settled target repairs are the sole exception to “every content edit is applied in Step 7”: they land before page certification can start, take the same verified-page dispositions, and are removed from Step 7's executable queue. This exception does not waive audit's autonomy condition — a support, provenance, citation-target, or relationship change is made only after the specific defect is fact-checked against the authoritative raw/source evidence. Every other content edit still lands in Step 7.

**Relationship-integrity barrier and verification epochs.** Warning-tier findings still enter audit as authored work, but a required missing target is locally certification-blocking. After Step 4 and before any page coverage probe or refuter, inspect every wikilink, raw-file target, and provenance/support target — not only rows inherited from lint — and classify every missing target under current `CLAUDE.md` and lint policy. Repair every positively-settled required maintained-body, raw-file, provenance, or support target; never guess a destination, create a placeholder page, remove support, or retarget a claim merely to clear the gate. Record every policy-permitted dangling link with its containing page, context, target, and exception class rather than changing it — including one-off collaborators in source-page `authors:` and genuine first-use future-term links. Provenance links never receive that exception. Then rerun consistency, the complete lint procedure including its LLM walk, and the whole-inventory relationship sweep. Missing, failed, empty, incomplete, or unparseable gate evidence blocks. Only zero unresolved required targets establishes `READY(n)`, the run's post-repair verification epoch; recompute the mode scope from the complete post-repair files and launch fresh blind refuters only from that epoch.

Any target, support/provenance, page-add/remove/rename, or relationship edit after `READY(n)` moves the run to `INVALIDATED`: stop launches, cancel/drain readers, reject late results, and discard every bullet/page verdict launched in that epoch plus its proof, promotion, marker-clearance, counts, and progress. Retain calls only as discarded cost. Rerun the barrier and rebuild scope/manifests. A new partial epoch may re-admit exact-valid rows only from a previously finalized committed terminal report; it never salvages the just-invalidated epoch. Full restarts every claim. Historical stamps outside recomputed partial scope remain governed by their hashes.

**Marker/hash mechanics.** An addition marked canonically as `> - *[unverified]* …` rides a `verified` page because the hash excludes that line; marker-shaped text elsewhere remains hashed. A *changed* existing claim demotes the page, because its line was in the hash and altering or masking it moves the hash. `body_hash.py` needs well-formed `---` frontmatter delimiters (Step 8).

**Canonicalize process markers before scope or hash decisions.** Immediately after creating the recovery report, scan maintained page bodies for every literal `*[unverified]*` token with Markdown context. If a token is genuinely serving as a pending-claim process marker but appears as a suffix or in any other noncanonical position, move that same token to immediately after the bullet's `> - ` prefix without changing the claim text. Do this before fresh lint, partial-scope classification, or any pre-hash comparison. On a `verified` page, the normalization itself does not authorize a re-stamp: record the preimage, force the page into the current partial scope, and treat its old stamp as non-clean until the ordinary claim/page gate settles it. Marker-shaped examples in inline/fenced code or HTML comments are literals, remain semantic and hashed, and are never moved or counted. If context does not establish whether a noncanonical token is literal documentation or real pending state, do not silently ignore or rewrite it: retain the bytes, record the exact occurrence pending, and finalize `incomplete` if the distinction cannot be settled autonomously.

## Arguments

`audit` accepts one optional positional argument selecting the verification scope: `partial` (default, equivalent to no argument) or `full` — see Verification Model for what each fact-checks. `/checkup` invokes audit in `partial` mode to preserve the claim-level optimization across routine runs.

Invoke as `audit`, `audit partial`, or `audit full`. These are the only valid forms. Reject any other token before creating a report or touching the wiki: echo it verbatim, state the three accepted forms, and stop. Never ask a scope question or silently coerce an invalid token to `partial`. After a valid invocation, audit asks no operational, continuation, budget, structural, graph, or follow-up question.

## When To Invoke

Use before high-stakes synthesis, paper drafting, major reference work, or after large ingests.

## When Not To Invoke

- A missing or stale lint/consistency report is not a reason to reject a valid invocation: audit runs the complete gate itself in Steps 1–2. A failed, empty, unparseable, or still-blocking gate produces an `incomplete` audit report with exact pending gate obligations and no page edit; it never becomes a request for the user to run another skill.
- Outstanding Warning-tier findings do not bar entry into audit — they are the authored worklist audit exists to carry out, at any backlog size (`references/apply-fixes.md`). This does not waive the relationship-integrity barrier: the required missing-target subset must be cleared at Step 4b before any page certification begins.
- The user wants a research answer. Use `query`.
- The wiki is too small to audit meaningfully.

## Procedure

Run every Python command below with the repository-supported default runtime (`python3` where that is the configured command); do not select an alternate interpreter ad hoc.

```text
Audit Progress:
- [ ] Step 1: Initialize the recovery report; run fresh lint; freeze every Warning identity
- [ ] Step 2: Run fresh consistency
- [ ] Step 3: Load memory; read wiki pages and raw sources
- [ ] Step 4: Run semantic checks; build the complete Warning/occurrence ledger
- [ ] Step 4a: Close verification-neutral and no-raw Warning work before verification
- [ ] Step 4b: Apply determinate relationship repairs; establish final `READY(n)`
- [ ] Step 5: Freeze current manifests; admit/backfill evidence on current final bodies
- [ ] Step 6: Fill the report's human findings view
- [ ] Step 7: Apply residual non-neutral fixes found by source verification; record the Worklist ledger
- [ ] Step 8: Verify rewritten bodies and set status — verification is terminal
- [ ] Step 9: Run fresh final lint; reconcile zero carried Warnings and pending; finalize report
```

1. **Initialize the recovery report, capture the baseline, canonicalize process markers, and run fresh lint.** Before any wiki or check-data edit, create the retained report from `references/report-template.md` with one non-placeholder run ID; record exact Git status/diff ownership, then run `.claude/skills/audit/scripts/capture_warning_baseline.py 2-outputs/audit/baselines/audit-{run-id}.json --repo-root . --run-id {run-id}`. The file must be new and exclusive. Require status 0 and retain its path/SHA, returned `baseline_id`, and `evidence_context_sha256`; all bind to the same run. The artifact freezes Warning/enumerator inventories, affected page preimages, ignore data, targets, and canonical/relationship rules. Perform the canonical-marker preflight and fresh complete lint, capturing status/stdout/stderr. Failed, empty, unparseable, or blocking evidence finalizes `incomplete` without further page edits.

   The fresh lint must be at least as new as the most recent `1-wiki/log.md` entry that touched a wiki page, with no uncommitted working-tree wiki edit unaccounted for — also run `git status --porcelain -- 1-wiki` (a hand edit in the editor need not have written a log entry and would otherwise ride through this gate against a drifted wiki). A non-empty git result is not itself the stop signal: no check commits mid-run, so the gating lint's own auto-fixes and its `log.md`/`hot.md`/`index.md` bookkeeping — and any page a prior audit edited before that lint in an `audit → lint → audit` cycle — are expected to sit uncommitted and are what the fresh lint accounts for. If a later unaccounted page edit appears, rerun the complete lint procedure against the current tree. A failed or blocking rerun makes the audit `incomplete` with the exact gate obligation; it never asks the user to rerun lint. (Step 5's body-hash self-check and per-page fact-check independently backstop a drifted page that slips past this gate.)

   Then collect lint's open authored-tier findings — **every Warning-tier finding in lint's report** (the ones lint detects but leaves for audit to carry out): e.g. missing reciprocal contradictions, length-cap review (a `length_cap_exceeded` page is split only when it genuinely holds more than one idea — length alone never forces a split, and a page long only from inline-citation density is not over the cap; `CLAUDE.md` → Length), source-context-phrase / vague-source-referent / `unlinked_page_mention` / mechanical AI-writing-tell rewrites, and bare page locators needing a `#page=N` deep-link (`page_locator_unlinked` — audit reopens the raw anyway, so it computes the printed→physical offset: `N = printed − (first_printed − 1)`; never guess offset zero on a source not paginated from 1). Take lint's Warning tier as the worklist, not a fixed sub-list, so a Warning lint adds later is still carried in. **Info-tier findings are not audit's to action** — in particular the user-owned rename/re-path checks `filename_not_kebab`, `source_stem_mismatch`, and `bare_basename_link`, whose only safe fix is a rename the user performs. A finding lint deterministically caught must be resolved even when audit's own Step 4 pass does not independently re-notice it — here the lint report is a worklist, not just a gate.

2. **Run fresh consistency next.** Run the complete consistency procedure against the current tree and capture status, stdout, and stderr separately. If it returns `result: findings` or `result: blocked`, fails, or produces empty/unparseable evidence, initialize or finalize the audit report as `incomplete`, record every exact unresolved schema-integrity obligation, stamp nothing, and stop without a question. `result: clean` can still carry `proposals:` > 0 (root-level proposals awaiting the user); those do not block audit. The fresh run must include committed and uncommitted changes to `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, and `.claude/skills/`, excluding only the two agent-writable runtime data files `multi-skill/hyphenation-lists.md` and `multi-skill/unlinked-mention-ignore.md`. Auditing against a drifted schema produces findings against rules the project no longer agrees on.

3. **Load memory, then read the wiki and raw sources.** Read both Audit and shared multi-skill memories, then the maintained wiki and its control pages. Retain the direct SHA-256 of every raw dependency. In `partial`, admit only a report-embedded raw pack that passes `references/verification-spec.md`; reopen every other scoped raw. In `full`, reopen all raws. Raw-pack reuse never preserves status, hashes, verdicts, or context-sensitive reasoning.

   Then run the shared ledger validator's raw-proof drift guard. A page may stay outside partial scope only when its exact current semantic generation and complete transitive raw path/SHA manifest match a committed terminal audit/ingest proof at Git HEAD: both page readers HOLD on that manifest, the status terminal is verified, and matching source rows exist. Dirty/untracked raw bytes, a changed manifest, or absence of such proof makes the page mandatory. Set an affected verified page `needs-update` with the raw-change/ingest-existing-source reason and surface a Critical; do not judge materiality or verify against replacement bytes.

4. **Run semantic checks** across all pages, verified or not — these are cross-page and prose-quality checks that need no raw re-read, so they apply even to pages Step 5 will skip. The full Critical / Warning / Info catalogue is in `references/semantic-checks.md` — run every check in it. Critical findings are support/faithfulness failures; Warnings are the authored-worklist items Steps 4a, 4b, and 7 carry out; Info are candidates and suggestions.

4a. **Close verification-neutral and no-raw Warning work before verification.** Follow `references/apply-fixes.md` and `references/unlinked-mention-occurrences.md`; use `.claude/skills/audit/scripts/enumerate_unlinked_mentions.py` and `.claude/skills/audit/scripts/build_suppression_batches.py`. Expand every candidate group into native-parity exact occurrences, apply standing ignores first, and finish the frozen worklist before `READY(n)`. Record one exact baseline-bound, non-overlapping `neutral_page_transactions` row for every page changed by one or more `genuine_wrap` occurrences; do not claim that executable row for a different allowlisted transform. A hash-stable `verified` host gets one pre-hash, all descending-offset edits, and one post-hash while remaining verified; another host preserves status and hash absence. A verified host with only de-hyphenation, spelling, or incidental-unwrapping work also preserves its status but stays in ordinary partial fact-check scope because it has no genuine-wrap raw-proof bridge; entering scope is not demotion. The Step 4a edit lane itself performs no raw read, verification reader, marker write, or demotion. Pack only suppression candidates into input-hash-bound consecutive maximal batches of 25 (last smaller) for exactly two blind readers; two HOLDs are required. Never substitute another span for a stale identity: record the old/new identity as a one-to-one superseded/rekeyed pair, rebuild affected batches, and reread. Rescan after each wave. A parity, packing, or execution failure ends `incomplete` with exact pending identities.

4b. **Clear the relationship-integrity barrier before verification.** Follow the Verification Model's barrier over the complete current inventory, not only the newest lint report's rows. Classify every missing wikilink, raw-file, and provenance/support target. Positively establish each required defect before editing: a stale path to the same logical page may be settled from canonical page identity; a support, provenance, citation-target, or relationship change requires the bounded raw/source fact-check and existing verified-page disposition in `references/apply-fixes.md`. For a graph-sensitive mention, follow `references/unlinked-mention-occurrences.md`: repair a confirmed genuine party, apply the same two-reader ignore quorum to a proven incidental non-party, and rescan. If the evidence does not determine the intended target or party status, make no relationship/body edit: preserve the body, set the page `needs-update` with the exact unresolved target choice, strip a stale hash, record the exact obligation pending, and finalize `incomplete` rather than guessing or asking a question. This Audit-specific no-guess branch governs Audit even while root `CLAUDE.md` still says to author a reciprocal when uncertain. Do not follow that root sentence, edit the root, or ask the user; record one outside-scope proposal in the report — `[cross-file] CLAUDE.md → Page Status: align uncertain reciprocity with Audit's evidence-gated no-edit / needs-update / incomplete disposition` — and continue under this rule. Keep every barrier item in the Worklist ledger, but mark each applied repair consumed so Step 7 cannot apply it again.

   After the determinate repairs, run fresh consistency, the complete lint procedure including its manual LLM walk, and `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/relationship-sweep.md` against the post-repair inventory. Fail closed on a missing report, non-zero/failed run, empty or unparseable output, incomplete manual walk, or any unresolved required target. If a gate or its fix changes a target or relationship, repeat this paragraph. Record allowed dangling links separately with page, context, target, and policy class. Only the final zero-required-target pass establishes `READY(n)`; pause all page-verification refuters until then.

5. **Freeze current manifests; admit/backfill evidence on current final bodies.** Start only after Step 4a has terminally reconciled the neutral worklist and Step 4b has established current `READY(n)`. Recompute mode scope and the residual content-changing worklist, and include every verified page targeted by a content-changing item. In `partial`, exclude a verified page whose only work was completed in Step 4a only when the shared validator admits its replay-valid neutral edge and exact current raw-proof closure; otherwise include it in ordinary fact-check scope without demoting it merely for the neutral edit. A draft/needs-update Step 4a host remains in ordinary fact-check scope. In `full`, retain every page because full-mode verification scope is unchanged. Write complete source/page/claim/bullet-role/page-reader/scanner/status-write manifests into the incomplete report created in Step 1. Inventory every logical bullet with full untruncated text and one terminal disposition. For every audit claim, record the current page's conservative full semantic-page digest as `context_digest`; the validator must independently match its retained page type, title, semantic frontmatter, callout type, callout block ID, and digest. Read `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/verification-ledger.md` directly. Treat this as the final manifest only while no later semantic edit changes an affected claim/page generation.

   In partial mode, admit only exact-valid `hold/hold` pairs from a committed clean terminal report and backfill both roles for every missing, invalid, legacy, changed, context-stale, or raw-stale occurrence. Never combine old and new roles. In full mode admit none and run both roles cold for every required claim. Run `validate_verification_ledger.py` after manifest construction and every repair. No result launched before `READY(n)` or in an invalidated current epoch is admissible.

   For each scoped page, follow `references/verification-spec.md`: coverage, per-page-type sections, claim support/faithfulness/reasons, exact citation pages, and yes/no assumptions. After all semantic edits land in Step 7 and the final epoch is current, compute the page generation with `validate_verification_ledger.py --page-generation {page}` and run two fresh blind whole-page readers over the complete final page/raw manifest tied to that digest: `locator_page` and `entailment_argument_page`. A page HOLD never fills a missing bullet row; either non-HOLD blocks promotion. A repair reruns both fresh page readers and both bullet roles for changed/new/revoked/dependent claims.

   **Guard an unstamped or stale-stamped verified page.** Treat a page marked `verified` with no `verified_hash:`, or whose hash does not match its body, as non-verified and fact-check it regardless of mode. Before skipping any `verified` page as unchanged in `partial` mode, run `python3 .claude/skills/multi-skill/scripts/body_hash.py {page}` yourself and compare to its `verified_hash:` — audit owns this check rather than assuming lint ran it this cycle. If `body_hash.py` is missing, exits non-zero, or returns a non-64-hex value (e.g. on malformed or unclosed-`---` frontmatter), do not skip the page and do not let the error abort the run: treat it as non-verified, fact-check it, repair the delimiters or set it `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` naming the malformed frontmatter, and surface a Critical.

   **If no non-verified pages and no `*[unverified]*` claims exist** (a fully-verified wiki with no pending markers, in `partial` mode), there is nothing to fact-check. Still run the Step 4 semantic checks across all pages, write the report with empty status-change sections, and note that the wiki was fully verified. Do not invent work to fill the pass.

6. **Fill the report's human findings view.** The report already exists from Step 1. Use `references/report-template.md` to add findings and non-blocking information without changing its current manifests. Status changes, verification proof, terminal numeric marker fields, reconciliation, and final `result` remain deferred to Step 9; never let the human view claim work the ledger has not terminally recorded.

   Record the run **Mode** (`partial` or `full`). Then judge whether a CLAUDE.md or style change warrants re-checking already-verified pages — a re-check is warranted for **one narrow case only**. Cheap gate first: run `git log -1 --format=%ad -- CLAUDE.md a-archive/style` (plus `git status --porcelain` over the same paths for uncommitted edits) against the most recent `full` audit in `2-outputs/audit/` history; if neither changed since, no note is due. If either did, read the diff. The **only** change a `partial` run cannot already reach on a verified page is a change to the *criterion by which an already-present, well-formed claim is judged faithful to or adequately supported by its raw source* — e.g. tightening the summary/aggregate rule so a claim that confirmed its one cited cell must now recompute the whole table. The claim still looks compliant to lint and to Step 4's semantic pass, so only re-opening the raw under the new criterion reveals the staleness. Bring every bounded affected page into the current partial scope and re-check it now. If the affected class cannot be bounded, finalize `incomplete` with the exact criterion and unbounded class rather than recommending another run. **Every other CLAUDE.md edit warrants no re-check,** because a cheaper mechanism already covers it: a format or locator-form change is a relocation (verification-neutral re-stamp); a required-callout or page-template change is structural (`lint`); a citation-presence, prose-style, atomicity, link, or redundancy rule is re-applied to every page — verified included — by Step 4 in the current run, and Step 7 fixes it against the raw; and a process or cosmetic edit (a skill procedure, the report shapes, the log/hot format, the directory tree, the memory tiers, a typo, a reword) alters no page at all.

7. **Apply the residual non-neutral fixes — autonomously.** For every page in Step 5's fact-check scope, act without asking the user; audit is the autonomy exception. Step 4a has already completed the neutral/no-raw lane without demotion. This step applies every remaining content edit after the repairs Step 4b consumed; Step 8 verifies the results and sets status. Read `references/apply-fixes.md` before editing any page — it holds the full mechanics:

   - **Confirm the defect before fixing it.** A false-positive fix is strictly worse than the defect it imagines, because audit overwrites correct content and certifies it, whereas a missed defect stays visible for the next run. Establish every defect positively against the raw before changing existing content — never from a pattern, an inference, or a prior report's say-so. The mis-located-citation exception, the four per-instance guards, and the yes/no audit-assumption framing for structural fixes are in the reference.
   - **Build the residual fix list** from audit's Step 4 findings and lint's Step 1 authored tier after removing Step 4a's terminal neutral rows and Step 4b's consumed repairs, and **resolve every remaining item this run** — deferral, batch size, run length, and spend are not dispositions.
   - **Determinate fixes** (a restated number, a distorted claim's wording, a missing citation the fact-check settles, a fabricated mechanism) apply inline; **authored fixes** (splits, merges, source-shaped rewrites) follow ingest's existing-source-mode and the `supersede` preservation/bookkeeping mechanics, inheriting the mechanics but not supersede's approval gates. Bullet removal escalates to a third refuter and requires all three to agree; a confirmed distortion audit cannot safely correct in-pass is set `needs-update` with a derivation (the distortion-disposition rule). A genuinely ambiguous structural call is preserved, set `needs-update` with the exact unresolved choice, and recorded as pending; if its Warning survives the final lint, the run is `incomplete`. After any citation fix, run the repeated-literal sweep across `1-wiki/`.
   - **Do not reopen Step 4a work.** A verified host whose only changes were allowlisted neutral transforms stays `verified`; a draft/needs-update host preserves that status and remains in scope. Step 7 does not raw-read, page-verify, mark, or demote either host for the neutral transforms. If a later content-changing defect is discovered on that page, it is a new non-neutral obligation and follows the ordinary disposition for its current status.
   - **Keep learned check-data writes in Step 4a.** Its exact paired-reader batch gate owns `hyphenation-lists.md` and `unlinked-mention-ignore.md`; never reopen that work here or touch `check_wiki.py` and its tests.
   - **Carry the markup** on anything you author or move: each callout its `> ^block-id`, each locator its `#page=N` deep-link in the form `CLAUDE.md` → Source Support And Verification defines for the page type (including the unpaginated-supplement exemption). Touch `updated:` on every page whose body audit edits; strip a stale `verified_hash:` on any page audit demotes.

   Step 7 ends with every residual item terminal and every content-changing target in current scope: a bounded hash-stable page is queued for delta-certification; an unbounded page is queued for complete-page certification; an uncertifiable page is `needs-update`, hashless, and marker-free. Nothing here is stamped. Step 4a neutral-only verified hosts remain separately re-stamped. Every semantic edit invalidates affected claim/page generations and manifest rows; rebuild them from the final body before final readers or status writes. A target, support/provenance, inventory/name, or relationship edit also invalidates the epoch: return to Step 4b and restart verification cold.

8. **Verify rewritten bodies and set status.** Verification is terminal: this is the last write each page receives. Follow `references/verify-and-set-status.md`:

   - **Final relationship-epoch gate.** Before any Step 8 reader or stamp, confirm current parseable `READY(n)`, zero unresolved required targets, no later graph edit, exact-valid bullet dispositions for every claim, and both fresh page-role rows for the final page generation. Otherwise return to Step 4b; current-epoch results become discarded cost and cannot be salvaged.
   - **Re-fact-check anything you rewrote.** A split, merge, source-shaped rewrite, or dropped bullet creates new evidence obligations. Run both bullet roles for every changed/new/revoked/dependent claim, then both fresh whole-page roles. Run semantic re-check, exact structural re-validation, and merge content conservation, bounded to three rounds; a residual/oscillating page becomes `needs-update` and the run is `unconverged` only after every planned unit has a terminal safe disposition.

## Worked Example

User invokes audit on a wiki of 14 concept/entity pages, 5 source pages, 1 synthesis. The newest lint report is clean except for two pages already marked `needs-update`.

The audit pass produces:

- **Critical (1):** [[1-wiki/concepts/positional-encoding.md|positional encoding]] lists [[1-wiki/sources/Vaswani2017AttentionIA.md|Vaswani2017AttentionIA]] in `sources:` but the source page does not support one of its bullets — the claim drifted in from a different source during a prior reingest. Proposed fix: drop the unsupported bullet and the bad sources entry.
- **Warning (2):** [[1-wiki/concepts/scaled-dot-product-attention.md|Scaled Dot-Product Attention]] covers two distinct ideas; recommend splitting. [[1-wiki/concepts/layer-norm.md|layer norm]] and [[1-wiki/concepts/layer-normalization.md|layer normalization]] are near-duplicate pages; recommend merging.
- **Info (1):** *verified candidate* — [[1-wiki/concepts/residual-connection.md|residual connection]] is supported by three source pages, has no open contradictions, reads in plain language.

Audit acts autonomously because it fact-checked each page against its raw. The positional-encoding claim's locator and entailment bullet readers both reject support; because deletion is destructive, a third reader reopens the raw by a different route before audit removes the bullet. The support-link change invalidates the epoch. Audit reruns the relationship barrier, recomputes scope, backfills any now-invalid bullet pairs, and runs fresh locator-page and entailment/argument-page readers on each final page before any stamp. The report retains every claim's terminal disposition, not only defects.

A second partial run exercises reuse and marker clearance. [[1-wiki/concepts/self-attention.md|self-attention]] carries one inherited `*[unverified]*` claim. Exact-valid committed pairs for unchanged claims are admitted; the pending claim receives both fresh bullet roles, and the complete page receives both fresh page roles. If all HOLD and the anchor is at HEAD, audit clears the marker and re-stamps. A reciprocal bullet with a new anchor cannot certify this run: audit sets the page `needs-update`, strips its hash, removes the process marker, and records the precise anchor obligation.

## Edge Cases

- **A note is accurate but too broad.** When the fact-check makes the split boundary clear, audit splits it following the `supersede` procedure rather than leaving one sprawling page in place. When the boundary is genuinely ambiguous, audit preserves the body, sets `needs-update` with the exact unresolved choice, and records the Warning identity as pending. The run cannot be `complete` while that Warning survives final lint.
- **A source page is wrong.** Audit sets the source page and the affected concept/entity pages `needs-update`; a full source-page rebuild from the raw is a job for ingest's existing-source mode, so audit flags that rather than re-extracting the raw itself.
- **A claim distorts the source by generalizing from the cell it cites.** A summary or comparison claim can be true of the single cell it cites yet false of the whole table — Step 5 recomputes the full set rather than confirming the cited cell (`references/verification-spec.md`). When confirmed, the faithful correction usually requires re-deriving the aggregate and often spans the source page and the pages that inherited the framing, so it is rarely a safe in-pass fix: set every affected page `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` carrying the recomputed derivation (the distortion-disposition rule, `references/apply-fixes.md`).
- **A page has no resolvable source.** A concept/entity page whose `sources:` is empty (or a synthesis with none) cannot be fact-checked, so it can never be set `verified`. Set it `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` naming the missing support. Audit does not auto-remove a page; if the structural Warning survives final lint, retain its exact identity and finalize `incomplete` without asking a question.
- **A contradiction is productive.** Preserve it; do not treat all contradictions as errors, and never resolve a contradiction by deleting one side.

## Limits

- Audit acts autonomously after a raw-source fact-check — it carries out content fixes (splits, merges, rewrites, support-link changes) following the `supersede` and ingest existing-source-mode procedures and preserving the prior version, then sets status. This autonomy is a deliberate departure from the foundation design's human-commit-point model (see Purpose). Its scope model, the terminal-verification invariant, and the marker mechanics are defined once in Verification Model.
- Do not audit from memory. Audit findings drive autonomous status and content changes, so they must be grounded in the current page content and in the raw source — not in recollection and not in the wiki's own pages alone.
- Never drop a bullet on a single fact-check. Bullet removal is the one default escalation to a third refuter, and requires all three to agree independently that the raw legibly does not support it; on any found support or disagreement, preserve the bullet — add the citation the support enables, or mark `*[tentative]*` and set `needs-update` — rather than deleting. Deletion is the last resort (`references/apply-fixes.md`).
- A confirmed defect always ends the run acted on — fixed when the correction is a safe in-pass determinate edit, set `needs-update` with a derivation when it is not (the distortion-disposition rule). A distortion audit confirms but cannot safely correct in-pass is never force-fixed with a risky rewrite and never left as a passive finding for the user. A genuinely ambiguous structural call is preserved and set `needs-update`; it is not handed back as a question, and any surviving Warning makes the run `incomplete`.
- Audit does not edit `CLAUDE.md`, skill files, or scripts. Schema or skill drift is `consistency`'s domain. The exceptions are the two agent-writable data files `.claude/skills/multi-skill/hyphenation-lists.md` and `.claude/skills/multi-skill/unlinked-mention-ignore.md`, which audit grows under the Step 4a two-reader gate — data, not script logic (`CLAUDE.md` → Stay In Your Lane). The folder holds a third data file, `.claude/skills/multi-skill/pagination-map.md`, which audit **reads** (a `p. M` locator is checked against it) but does **not** maintain — it is registered on ingest after a human eyeballs a rendered footer. An audit that meets an unregistered raw reports it (`pagination_map_unregistered`) rather than guessing its pagination.
- A `verified` stamp certifies the page body against the raw *as read at verification time*. `verified_hash:` binds the page body, not the raw, so a raw silently re-OCR'd, re-curated, or replaced under the same stem after verification is not detected. This rests on the `0-raw/` immutability invariant. When a fact-check notices the raw diverging from what a page records (offsets shifted, sections renumbered, different text at a cited `#page=N`), set the page `needs-update` naming the suspected raw change rather than re-verifying against it.
- Never modify `0-raw/`.
