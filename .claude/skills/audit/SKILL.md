# audit

Audit is the judgement pass. It asks whether the notes are good thinking objects.

## Purpose

Structural lint can say whether a page has the right sections. Audit says whether a concept/entity page is one idea, understandable, supported, and useful, and it earns the `verified` stamp by fact-checking the page against its raw source.

`verified` here means fact-checked against the raw source and passed. It is not the Ahrens-style human re-voicing gate that the foundation design (`a-archive/reference/smart-notes-llm-wiki-integration.md`) assigns to promotion. This wiki deliberately relocates that safeguard: pages stay LLM-drafted, and the defence against AI-voice prose lives in the ingest note-quality packet plus audit's own semantic-tell check (Step 4), not in a human rewrite at promotion. Audit's autonomy is therefore a conscious departure from that document's human-commit-point model, authorized by `CLAUDE.md`. Do not weaken the Step 4 tell-check or the ingest packet without re-introducing a human re-voicing step in their place.

## Verification Model

The scope, marker, and status rules every step below relies on, defined once here. The full runtime statement is `CLAUDE.md` → Page Status and Bullet Markers; this is audit's operating summary.

Five load-bearing terms:

- `verified_hash:` — the SHA-256 of a page body with `*[unverified]*` claim lines excluded. It guarantees the page's unmarked content is byte-identical to what was last fact-checked: immutability since the check, not correctness of the check. Re-confirm correctness with `full` mode.
- `*[unverified]*` — a claim-level marker on a non-obvious claim added since the last fact-check (or one whose backing image was re-extracted without a text change), pending a raw re-check. A *changed* claim demotes the page rather than riding it, so a marker on a `verified` page is an addition, not an edit. It is the unit `partial` mode re-checks, and the hash excludes it so it can ride a `verified` page as the pending delta.
- `*[tentative]*` — a claim-level marker for weak or thin support. Unlike `*[unverified]*` it is an epistemic judgement, not a process state: a fact-check does not clear it, and the two can co-occur on one claim. It is the safe-failure marker audit falls back to whenever the raw cannot positively settle a claim's support — mark `*[tentative]*` and set `needs-update` rather than delete or self-stamp.
- authored-tier worklist — the Warning-tier findings lint detects but leaves for audit to carry out. Audit's authored worklist *is* lint's Warning output, so a Warning check lint adds later automatically becomes audit's job with no edit here.
- durable bullet evidence — the exact-valid locator/entailment pair and per-claim terminal row defined in `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/verification-ledger.md`. It certifies only its exact bullet/locator/raw/context generation. It never substitutes for audit's fresh whole-page pair or preserves a page stamp across a support change.

**Scope by mode.** `partial` (default) includes every non-verified page plus every `*[unverified]*` claim on an otherwise-`verified` page and any page current defects/raw invalidation bring into scope. It may reuse exact-valid bullet pairs and backfills only missing/invalid pairs; every scoped page still pays two fresh page readers. Marker-free verified pages remain outside partial scope. `full` includes every page and deliberately distrusts prior bullet evidence: every required claim receives a new locator/entailment pair (`reused = 0`) plus both fresh page readers. Use `full` for a broad criterion change, a rushed prior audit, or periodic deep confirmation. A verified page whose cold re-check holds keeps its hash/date unless a correction or marker clearance requires a terminal write.

**Verification is terminal.** The `verified` stamp is the last write a page receives. Every content edit — audit's own Step 4 findings, lint's Step 1 authored worklist, and any cross-page edit landing on the page (a reciprocal mirror, an inherited-distortion fix) — is applied in Step 7, *before* the page is re-verified and stamped in Step 8. No fix ever lands on an already-stamped page. A content-changing worklist item on a `verified` page takes one of the four dispositions in `references/apply-fixes.md` — delta-certify, fix-and-mask, verify-in-scope, or fix-and-demote; only the last two bring the page into this run's whole-page fact-check scope, and the first two keep the stamp with the re-check scoped to the changed claims plus every aggregate claim on the page and any bullet the delta-collateral sweep implicates (`CLAUDE.md` → Page Status, the audit claim-scoped dispositions). The hard invariant: **a page never keeps or earns `verified` while a content-changing worklist item on it is unapplied or its post-edit body unverified.** The edits themselves never stage across runs; the closed list of the two costs that may, and the ledger each staged item owes, are in `references/apply-fixes.md` → Build The Fix List And Resolve The Authored Worklist — under the same invariant (a page whose edit landed but whose re-verification could not finish stays `draft`/`needs-update`, and the next `partial` audit re-verifies it).

Step 4b's positively-settled target repairs are the sole exception to “every content edit is applied in Step 7”: they land before page certification can start, take the same verified-page dispositions, and are removed from Step 7's executable queue. This exception does not waive audit's autonomy condition — a support, provenance, citation-target, or relationship change is made only after the specific defect is fact-checked against the authoritative raw/source evidence. Every other content edit still lands in Step 7.

**Relationship-integrity barrier and verification epochs.** Warning-tier findings still enter audit as authored work, but a required missing target is locally certification-blocking. After Step 4 and before any page coverage probe or refuter, inspect every wikilink, raw-file target, and provenance/support target — not only rows inherited from lint — and classify every missing target under current `CLAUDE.md` and lint policy. Repair every positively-settled required maintained-body, raw-file, provenance, or support target; never guess a destination, create a placeholder page, remove support, or retarget a claim merely to clear the gate. Record every policy-permitted dangling link with its containing page, context, target, and exception class rather than changing it — including one-off collaborators in source-page `authors:` and genuine first-use future-term links. Provenance links never receive that exception. Then rerun consistency, the complete lint procedure including its LLM walk, and the whole-inventory relationship sweep. Missing, failed, empty, incomplete, or unparseable gate evidence blocks. Only zero unresolved required targets establishes `READY(n)`, the run's post-repair verification epoch; recompute the mode scope from the complete post-repair files and launch fresh blind refuters only from that epoch.

Any target, support/provenance, page-add/remove/rename, or relationship edit after `READY(n)` moves the run to `INVALIDATED`: stop launches, cancel/drain readers, reject late results, and discard every bullet/page verdict launched in that epoch plus its proof, promotion, marker-clearance, counts, and progress. Retain calls only as discarded cost. Rerun the barrier and rebuild scope/manifests. A new partial epoch may re-admit exact-valid rows only from a previously finalized committed terminal report; it never salvages the just-invalidated epoch. Full restarts every claim. Historical stamps outside recomputed partial scope remain governed by their hashes.

**Marker/hash mechanics.** An addition marked `*[unverified]*` rides a `verified` page because the hash excludes it; a *changed* existing claim demotes the page, because its line was in the hash and altering or masking it moves the hash. `body_hash.py` masks `*[unverified]*` lines before hashing and needs well-formed `---` frontmatter delimiters (Step 8).

## Arguments

`audit` accepts one optional positional argument selecting the verification scope: `partial` (default, equivalent to no argument) or `full` — see Verification Model for what each fact-checks. `/checkup` invokes audit in `partial` mode to preserve the claim-level optimization across routine runs.

Invoke as `audit` or `audit full`. If the token looks like a page path or name (contains `/`, ends in `.md`, or matches an existing page title), the caller likely wants to scope audit to one page — but audit has no single-page or target mode and would otherwise run an autonomous whole-wiki pass, so confirm scope via `AskUserQuestion` before proceeding rather than disclosing the ignored token only in the after-the-fact report. For any other unrecognized token, do not start the autonomous whole-wiki pass. Echo the token verbatim and ask whether the user intended `partial` or `full`; proceed only after the scope is resolved. Never silently coerce an unknown token to `partial`.

## When To Invoke

Use before high-stakes synthesis, paper drafting, major reference work, or after large ingests.

## When Not To Invoke

- No recent lint report exists. Run `lint` first.
- Lint has unresolved critical structural findings other than expected `needs-update` pages. Outstanding Warning-tier findings do not bar entry into audit — they are the authored worklist audit exists to carry out, at any backlog size (`references/apply-fixes.md`). This does not waive the relationship-integrity barrier: the required missing-target subset must be cleared at Step 4b before any page certification begins.
- No recent consistency report exists, or the project has changed `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, or `.claude/skills/` since the last clean consistency run. Run `consistency` first — auditing pages against a drifted schema produces findings against rules the project no longer agrees on.
- The user wants a research answer. Use `query`.
- The wiki is too small to audit meaningfully.

## Procedure

```text
Audit Progress:
- [ ] Step 1: Verify lint; collect its authored-tier worklist
- [ ] Step 2: Verify consistency is clean and recent
- [ ] Step 3: Load memory; read wiki pages and raw sources
- [ ] Step 4: Run semantic checks
- [ ] Step 4b: Clear the relationship-integrity barrier; establish `READY(n)`
- [ ] Step 5: Freeze final manifests; initialize the report; admit/backfill bullet evidence; run page readers
- [ ] Step 6: Fill the report's human findings view
- [ ] Step 7: Apply the remaining content edits — verified pages a content edit targets come into scope; record the Worklist ledger
- [ ] Step 8: Verify rewritten bodies and set status — verification is terminal
- [ ] Step 9: Finalize report; update index, hot, and log
```

1. **Verify lint first.** Read the newest `2-outputs/lint/lint-*.md` and gate on its frontmatter `result:` field. If `result: blocking` (equivalently `audit_blocking:` > 0), stop and ask the user to clear the findings under the report's `## Audit-Blocking` section. `result: clean` means no audit-blocking finding remains — lint excludes the standing repo-state items audit cannot act on (`raw_without_source_page`, `uningested_raw_source`, `status_needs_update`) from the blocking count, so a clean lint can still carry those Criticals. If the report predates this field, classify manually: a Critical is audit-blocking unless its `check_id` is one of those three; Warnings and Info never change lint's entry-gate result; when a critical's class is unclear, treat it as blocking. The required missing-target Warning subset still blocks page certification locally at Step 4b.

   **Recent** means the lint is at least as new as the most recent `1-wiki/log.md` entry that touched a wiki page, with no uncommitted working-tree wiki edit unaccounted for — also run `git status --porcelain -- 1-wiki` (a hand edit in the editor need not have written a log entry and would otherwise ride through this gate against a drifted wiki). A non-empty git result is not itself the stop signal: no check commits mid-run, so the gating lint's own auto-fixes and its `log.md`/`hot.md`/`index.md` bookkeeping — and any page a prior audit edited before that lint in an `audit → lint → audit` cycle — are expected to sit uncommitted and are what the newest lint report accounts for. Stop only on an uncommitted `1-wiki` change the pipeline does not account for: a wiki page whose content changed after the gating lint, named in neither that lint report's Auto-Fixed list nor a wiki-touching `log.md` entry at or before its timestamp (a genuine post-lint hand edit). Then stop and ask the user to re-run lint. (Step 5's body-hash self-check and per-page fact-check independently backstop a drifted page that slips past this gate.)

   Then collect lint's open authored-tier findings — **every Warning-tier finding in lint's report** (the ones lint detects but leaves for audit to carry out): e.g. missing reciprocal contradictions, length-cap review (a `length_cap_exceeded` page is split only when it genuinely holds more than one idea — length alone never forces a split, and a page long only from inline-citation density is not over the cap; `CLAUDE.md` → Length), source-context-phrase / vague-source-referent / `unlinked_page_mention` / mechanical AI-writing-tell rewrites, and bare page locators needing a `#page=N` deep-link (`page_locator_unlinked` — audit reopens the raw anyway, so it computes the printed→physical offset: `N = printed − (first_printed − 1)`; never guess offset zero on a source not paginated from 1). Take lint's Warning tier as the worklist, not a fixed sub-list, so a Warning lint adds later is still carried in. **Info-tier findings are not audit's to action** — in particular the user-owned rename/re-path checks `filename_not_kebab`, `source_stem_mismatch`, and `bare_basename_link`, whose only safe fix is a rename the user performs. A finding lint deterministically caught must be resolved even when audit's own Step 4 pass does not independently re-notice it — here the lint report is a worklist, not just a gate.

2. **Verify consistency next.** Read the newest `2-outputs/consistency/consistency-*.md` and gate on its `result:` field the same way. If `result: findings` or `result: blocked`, stop and ask the user to clear consistency. `result: clean` can still carry `proposals:` > 0 (root-level proposals awaiting the user); those do not block audit. If the report predates this field, fall back to the prose check: if any unresolved findings remain in the body, stop. **Recent** means the consistency report is at least as new as the most recent change to `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, or `.claude/skills/` — check via `git log -1 --format=%ad -- CLAUDE.md README.md .claude/skills ':(exclude).claude/skills/multi-skill/hyphenation-lists.md' ':(exclude).claude/skills/multi-skill/unlinked-mention-ignore.md'`, and also `git status --porcelain` over that same pathspec for uncommitted edits the git-log date misses. The two excluded paths are the agent-writable shared data files audit itself grows in Step 7 (runtime data, already exempt from consistency's content scan), so audit's own sanctioned writes to them are never grounds to re-run consistency; every other `.claude/skills` change (and `pagination-map.md`) still trips the gate. If those committed changes postdate the last consistency run, or any uncommitted change to them exists, stop and ask the user to re-run consistency. Auditing against a drifted schema produces findings against rules the project no longer agrees on.

3. **Load memory, then read the wiki and the raw sources.** First read `.claude/skills/audit/audit-memory.md` and `.claude/skills/multi-skill/multi-skill-memory.md` to apply prior corrections about scope, fact-check aggressiveness, and which judgement calls audit has been told to make differently. Then read source pages, concept/entity pages, syntheses, `hot.md`, `index.md`, and `log.md`. For every raw dependency, compute and retain the direct SHA-256 of current bytes; Git timestamps remain a drift signal, never evidence identity. In `partial`, classify each raw before opening it. Reuse only a report-embedded raw pack admitted under `references/verification-spec.md`; reopen every new, changed, legacy, incomplete, mismatched, or otherwise invalid raw. In `full`, reopen every raw. Raw-pack reuse never preserves a page status, `verified_hash:`, bullet verdict, page verdict, or context-sensitive reasoning. A marker-free verified page needs no raw re-read in partial mode unless another guard brings it into scope.

   **Then check whether any raw changed under a stamp already resting on it.** `verified_hash:` binds the page body, not the raw (Limits), so a raw re-OCR'd, re-curated, or replaced under the same stem after verification is otherwise invisible — and the pages most exposed are exactly the marker-free `verified` ones this step does *not* reopen. Git already fingerprints every committed raw, so this needs no stored state: for each raw backing at least one `verified` page, run `git status --porcelain -- <raw>` and `git log -1 --format=%cI -- <raw>`. A modified or staged raw, or a last-commit timestamp later than the `updated:` of a `verified` page citing it, means that page's stamp rests on bytes that are no longer there. Set only those pages `needs-update` — a `draft` or `needs-update` page is being checked against current bytes anyway — with `needs_update_reason: raw {stem} changed since verification; reingest to re-baseline, then the next partial re-verifies`, and surface it as a Critical. Do not re-verify against the new bytes, and do not judge whether the change was material: a deliberate replacement (a better scan) clears in one reingest, which re-baselines the page and applies the properly materiality-judged cascade. An untracked raw, or one with no history, is reported as "cannot establish" and demotes nothing — the same fail-safe disposition as an unregistered pagination map.

4. **Run semantic checks** across all pages, verified or not — these are cross-page and prose-quality checks that need no raw re-read, so they apply even to pages Step 5 will skip. The full Critical / Warning / Info catalogue is in `references/semantic-checks.md` — run every check in it. Critical findings are support/faithfulness failures; Warnings are the authored-worklist items Steps 4b and 7 carry out; Info are candidates and suggestions.

4b. **Clear the relationship-integrity barrier before verification.** Follow the Verification Model's barrier over the complete current inventory, not only the newest lint report's rows. Classify every missing wikilink, raw-file, and provenance/support target. Positively establish each required defect before editing: a stale path to the same logical page may be settled from canonical page identity; a support, provenance, citation-target, or relationship change requires the bounded raw/source fact-check and existing verified-page disposition in `references/apply-fixes.md`. If the evidence does not determine the intended target, make no edit and stop blocked rather than guessing. Keep every barrier item in the Worklist ledger, but mark each applied repair consumed so Step 7 cannot apply it again.

   After the determinate repairs, run fresh consistency, the complete lint procedure including its manual LLM walk, and `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/relationship-sweep.md` against the post-repair inventory. Fail closed on a missing report, non-zero/failed run, empty or unparseable output, incomplete manual walk, or any unresolved required target. If a gate or its fix changes a target or relationship, repeat this paragraph. Record allowed dangling links separately with page, context, target, and policy class. Only the final zero-required-target pass establishes `READY(n)`; pause all page-verification refuters until then.

5. **Freeze manifests; initialize the report; admit/backfill evidence; run page readers.** Start only from current `READY(n)`. Recompute mode scope and the residual content worklist, include every verified page targeted by a content-changing item, and freeze complete source/page/claim/bullet-role/page-reader/scanner/status-write manifests. Before dispatch, create the retained Step 6 report with `result: incomplete` and nonnumeric `markers_pending: pending` / `inherited_cleared: pending`; `check_audit_burndown` deliberately skips these template values. Inventory every logical bullet with full untruncated text and one terminal disposition. Read `.claude/skills/multi-skill/references/verification-ledger.md` directly.

   In partial mode, admit only exact-valid `hold/hold` pairs from a committed clean terminal report and backfill both roles for every missing, invalid, legacy, changed, context-stale, or raw-stale occurrence. Never combine old and new roles. In full mode admit none and run both roles cold for every required claim. Run `validate_verification_ledger.py` after manifest construction and every repair. No result launched before `READY(n)` or in an invalidated current epoch is admissible.

   For each scoped page, follow `references/verification-spec.md`: coverage, per-page-type sections, claim support/faithfulness/reasons, exact citation pages, and yes/no assumptions. After all semantic edits land in Step 7 and the final epoch is current, run two fresh blind whole-page readers over the complete final page/raw manifest: `locator_page` and `entailment_argument_page`. A page HOLD never fills a missing bullet row; either non-HOLD blocks promotion. A repair reruns both fresh page readers and both bullet roles for changed/new/revoked/dependent claims.

   **Guard an unstamped or stale-stamped verified page.** Treat a page marked `verified` with no `verified_hash:`, or whose hash does not match its body, as non-verified and fact-check it regardless of mode. Before skipping any `verified` page as unchanged in `partial` mode, run `python3 .claude/skills/multi-skill/scripts/body_hash.py {page}` yourself and compare to its `verified_hash:` — audit owns this check rather than assuming lint ran it this cycle. If `body_hash.py` is missing, exits non-zero, or returns a non-64-hex value (e.g. on malformed or unclosed-`---` frontmatter), do not skip the page and do not let the error abort the run: treat it as non-verified, fact-check it, repair the delimiters or set it `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` naming the malformed frontmatter, and surface a Critical.

   **If no non-verified pages and no `*[unverified]*` claims exist** (a fully-verified wiki with no pending markers, in `partial` mode), there is nothing to fact-check. Still run the Step 4 semantic checks across all pages, write the report with empty status-change sections, and note that the wiki was fully verified. Do not invent work to fill the pass.

6. **Fill the report's human findings view.** The report already exists from Step 5. Use `references/report-template.md` to add findings and recommendations without changing its frozen manifests. Status changes, verification proof, terminal numeric marker fields, reconciliation, and final `result` remain deferred to Step 9; never let the human view claim work the ledger has not terminally recorded.

   Record the run **Mode** (`partial` or `full`). Then judge whether a CLAUDE.md or style change warrants re-checking already-verified pages — a re-check is warranted for **one narrow case only**. Cheap gate first: run `git log -1 --format=%ad -- CLAUDE.md a-archive/style` (plus `git status --porcelain` over the same paths for uncommitted edits) against the most recent `full` audit in `2-outputs/audit/` history; if neither changed since, no note is due. If either did, read the diff. The **only** change a `partial` run cannot already reach on a verified page is a change to the *criterion by which an already-present, well-formed claim is judged faithful to or adequately supported by its raw source* — e.g. tightening the summary/aggregate rule so a claim that confirmed its one cited cell must now recompute the whole table. The claim still looks compliant to lint and to Step 4's semantic pass, so only re-opening the raw under the new criterion reveals the staleness. For that case only, note under **Recommendations** that the affected verified pages need re-checking against their raw: prefer a targeted re-check — demote just those pages so the next `partial` re-verifies them — and recommend a whole-wiki `full` only when the affected class cannot be bounded. **Every other CLAUDE.md edit warrants no re-check,** because a cheaper mechanism already covers it: a format or locator-form change is a relocation (verification-neutral re-stamp); a required-callout or page-template change is structural (`lint`); a citation-presence, prose-style, atomicity, link, or redundancy rule is re-applied to every page — verified included — by Step 4 on the next `partial`, and Step 7 fixes it against the raw; and a process or cosmetic edit (a skill procedure, the report shapes, the log/hot format, the directory tree, the memory tiers, a typo, a reword) alters no page at all. Note such a change in passing without recommending a re-check.

7. **Apply the remaining fixes — autonomously.** For every page in Step 5's fact-check scope, act without asking the user; audit is the autonomy exception. This step applies every remaining content edit after the repairs Step 4b consumed; Step 8 verifies the results and sets status. Read `references/apply-fixes.md` before editing any page — it holds the full mechanics:

   - **Confirm the defect before fixing it.** A false-positive fix is strictly worse than the defect it imagines, because audit overwrites correct content and certifies it, whereas a missed defect stays visible for the next run. Establish every defect positively against the raw before changing existing content — never from a pattern, an inference, or a prior report's say-so. The mis-located-citation exception, the four per-instance guards, and the yes/no audit-assumption framing for structural fixes are in the reference.
   - **Build the residual fix list** from audit's Step 4 findings and lint's Step 1 authored tier after removing Step 4b's consumed repairs, and **resolve every item this run** — deferral and batch size are not dispositions (the reference gives the per-check dispositions for `missing_reciprocal_contradiction`, `concept_source_asymmetry`, `unlinked_page_mention`, `stale_mention_ignore`, `intra_page_redundancy`, and the `vague_source_referent` / `source_context_leak` triage, and the bounded staged valve).
   - **Determinate fixes** (a restated number, a distorted claim's wording, a missing citation the fact-check settles, a fabricated mechanism) apply inline; **authored fixes** (splits, merges, source-shaped rewrites) follow ingest's existing-source-mode and the `supersede` preservation/bookkeeping mechanics, inheriting the mechanics but not supersede's approval gates. Bullet removal escalates to a third refuter and requires all three to agree; a confirmed distortion audit cannot safely correct in-pass is set `needs-update` with a derivation (the distortion-disposition rule); a genuinely ambiguous structural call is downgraded to a Warning for the user. After any citation fix, run the repeated-literal sweep across `1-wiki/`.
   - **Verification-neutral worklist fixes on out-of-scope verified pages** (an `unlinked_page_mention` wrap, a de-hyphenation, a spelling fix, an incidental reciprocity-link unwrap) re-stamp `verified_hash:` with `body_hash.py` rather than demoting — but only after `body_hash.py` confirms the page still matches its stamp. The four format fixes (`callout_block_id`, `wikilink_pipe_spacing`, `citation_bracket_style`, `embed_not_isolated`) are lint's, not audit's.
   - **Grow the check data lists** — `hyphenation-lists.md` and `unlinked-mention-ignore.md` — autonomously, but write no entry on audit's lone judgement: each addition is sub-agent-verified by two independent readers that must both confirm, each reading its own ground truth; a split means the entry is not added, never a third vote to break it. Never touch `check_wiki.py` or its tests. In a fanned-out run these appends and the central locator-format pass are deferred to the central post-fan-out pass (Step 8), spawned from the top-level agent, so parallel writes never collide.
   - **Carry the markup** on anything you author or move: each callout its `> ^block-id`, each locator its `#page=N` deep-link in the form `CLAUDE.md` → Source Support And Verification defines for the page type (including the unpaginated-supplement exemption). Touch `updated:` on every page whose body audit edits; strip a stale `verified_hash:` on any page audit demotes.

   Step 7 ends with every in-scope page edited and every `verified` page a content edit targeted either holding a claim-scoped disposition (delta-certify / fix-and-mask, its delta queued for Step 8) or demoted into scope — nothing stamped. Any Step 7 edit that changes a target, support/provenance link, page inventory/name, or relationship invalidates the current epoch: finish that determinate edit, then return to Step 4b, rerun its gates, recompute scope, and restart verification cold before any stamp.

8. **Verify rewritten bodies and set status.** Verification is terminal: this is the last write each page receives. Follow `references/verify-and-set-status.md`:

   - **Final relationship-epoch gate.** Before any Step 8 reader or stamp, confirm current parseable `READY(n)`, zero unresolved required targets, no later graph edit, exact-valid bullet dispositions for every claim, and both fresh page-role rows for the final page generation. Otherwise return to Step 4b; current-epoch results become discarded cost and cannot be salvaged.
   - **Re-fact-check anything you rewrote.** A split, merge, source-shaped rewrite, or dropped bullet creates new evidence obligations. Run both bullet roles for every changed/new/revoked/dependent claim, then both fresh whole-page roles. Run semantic re-check, exact structural re-validation, and merge content conservation, bounded to three rounds; a residual/oscillating page becomes `needs-update` and the run is `unconverged` only after every planned unit has a terminal safe disposition.
   - **Central locator-format pass** (after a fanned-out audit, before any stamp): centrally re-verify every `#page=N` and section anchor against the raw, normalize the citation format, and apply all shared-file writes and the data-list quorum from this top-level agent, never nested in a fan-out subagent.
   - **Execution order:** (1) apply fixes; (2) establish final epoch; (3) reconcile bullet evidence; (4) run both fresh page roles; (5) semantic/structural/conservation checks; (6) central locator pass; (7) terminal mechanical marker/status/hash write; (8) validate reconciliation.
   - **Set `verified`** only when coverage holds, every required claim has an exact valid pair, both final page roles HOLD, and all other gates pass. Retained claim evidence authorizes only the bullet layer. Marker clearance additionally requires its anchor at Git HEAD, current page HOLDs, post-write structural success, and a pre/post check proving the terminal write removed only `*[unverified]*` and updated status/hash. A page with a residual support/faithfulness/coverage/contradiction problem becomes `needs-update` with a precise reason. Never resolve a contradiction by deleting one side.

9. **Finalize the report; update index, hot, and log.** Reconcile every planned source, raw acquisition, page, claim, bullet role, page reader, scanner item, and status write against the final epoch. Require exact raw arithmetic and `pending_raws = 0` before any terminal result. If runnable pending work remains while active readers are zero, dispatch a backstop before adjudicating. Replace the nonnumeric marker placeholders only at finalization. Run `validate_verification_ledger.py`; `complete` requires every planned item terminal and `pending = 0`, while bounded terminal repair failure is `unconverged` and missing/unparseable/undispatched/stale work is `incomplete`. Never count “source waved” as its claims read. An incomplete or unconverged run emits no normal success language.

   Then reconcile the human view: record actual status outcomes, drop non-verified pages from Verification proof, and count invalidated-epoch work only as discarded cost. Close the Worklist arithmetic, name inherited-marker blockers, update index for page additions/removals, and apply dependent-cascade source bookkeeping. A source addition atomically demotes to `draft`; a removal leaving lost/stale support becomes `needs-update`; both strip the old hash. Update hot/log only for a complete run; an incomplete/unconverged report remains the recovery record. A fresh full lint is still owed before the next audit.

The audit pass produces:

- **Warning (2):** [[1-wiki/concepts/scaled-dot-product-attention.md|Scaled Dot-Product Attention]] covers two distinct ideas; recommend splitting. Audit acts autonomously because it fact-checked each page against its raw. The positional-encoding claim's locator and entailment bullet readers both reject support; because deletion is destructive, a third reader reopens the raw by a different route before audit removes the bullet. ## Edge Cases

- **A note is accurate but too broad.** When the fact-check makes the split boundary clear, audit splits it following the `supersede` procedure rather than leaving one sprawling page in place. When the boundary is genuinely ambiguous, audit downgrades to a Warning and leaves the page for the user.
- **A source page is wrong.** Audit sets the source page and the affected concept/entity pages `needs-update`; a full source-page rebuild from the raw is a job for ingest's existing-source mode, so audit flags that rather than re-extracting the raw itself.
- **A claim distorts the source by generalizing from the cell it cites.** A summary or comparison claim can be true of the single cell it cites yet false of the whole table — Step 5 recomputes the full set rather than confirming the cited cell (`references/verification-spec.md`). When confirmed, the faithful correction usually requires re-deriving the aggregate and often spans the source page and the pages that inherited the framing, so it is rarely a safe in-pass fix: set every affected page `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` carrying the recomputed derivation (the distortion-disposition rule, `references/apply-fixes.md`).
- **A page has no resolvable source.** A concept/entity page whose `sources:` is empty (or a synthesis with none) cannot be fact-checked, so it can never be set `verified`. Set it `needs-update` with a `needs_update_reason:` naming the missing support, or surface removal as a Warning for the user (`forget` territory); audit does not auto-remove a page.
- **A contradiction is productive.** Preserve it; do not treat all contradictions as errors, and never resolve a contradiction by deleting one side.

## Limits

- Audit acts autonomously after a raw-source fact-check — it carries out content fixes (splits, merges, rewrites, support-link changes) following the `supersede` and ingest existing-source-mode procedures and preserving the prior version, then sets status. This autonomy is a deliberate departure from the foundation design's human-commit-point model (see Purpose). Its scope model, the terminal-verification invariant, and the marker mechanics are defined once in Verification Model.
- Do not audit from memory. Audit findings drive autonomous status and content changes, so they must be grounded in the current page content and in the raw source — not in recollection and not in the wiki's own pages alone.
- Never drop a bullet on a single fact-check. Bullet removal is the one default escalation to a third refuter, and requires all three to agree independently that the raw legibly does not support it; on any found support or disagreement, preserve the bullet — add the citation the support enables, or mark `*[tentative]*` and set `needs-update` — rather than deleting. Deletion is the last resort (`references/apply-fixes.md`).
- A confirmed defect always ends the run acted on — fixed when the correction is a safe in-pass determinate edit, set `needs-update` with a derivation when it is not (the distortion-disposition rule). A distortion audit confirms but cannot safely correct in-pass is never force-fixed with a risky rewrite and never left as a passive finding for the user. The only confirmed defect handed back as a bare Warning is a genuinely ambiguous structural call audit cannot settle.
- Audit does not edit `CLAUDE.md`, skill files, or scripts. Schema or skill drift is `consistency`'s domain. The exceptions are the two agent-writable data files `.claude/skills/multi-skill/hyphenation-lists.md` and `.claude/skills/multi-skill/unlinked-mention-ignore.md`, which audit grows under the Step 7 sub-agent-verification gate — data, not script logic (`CLAUDE.md` → Stay In Your Lane). The folder holds a third data file, `.claude/skills/multi-skill/pagination-map.md`, which audit **reads** (a `p. M` locator is checked against it) but does **not** maintain — it is registered on ingest after a human eyeballs a rendered footer. An audit that meets an unregistered raw reports it (`pagination_map_unregistered`) rather than guessing its pagination.
- A `verified` stamp certifies the page body against the raw *as read at verification time*. `verified_hash:` binds the page body, not the raw, so a raw silently re-OCR'd, re-curated, or replaced under the same stem after verification is not detected. This rests on the `0-raw/` immutability invariant. When a fact-check notices the raw diverging from what a page records (offsets shifted, sections renumbered, different text at a cited `#page=N`), set the page `needs-update` naming the suspected raw change rather than re-verifying against it.
- Never modify `0-raw/`.
